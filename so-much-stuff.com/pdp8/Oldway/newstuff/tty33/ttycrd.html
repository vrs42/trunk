These are my replacements for the DEC W076 and M452 modules.

The W076x provides connection from a PDP-8/i or PDP-8/L to an RS-232 or a current loop device.

The M452x provides easily selectable baud rates up to 19200.

I am making these cards available to help hobbyists with PDP-8/I and PDP-8/L computers get them interfaced to modern PCs or terminals.
