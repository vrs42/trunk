PDP-8/L 

I bought my first PDP-8/L from Charles Morris in September of 2003, along with my first ASR-33 TTY, and Charles' prototype DF32 disk emulator. (It is pictured here on Charles' bench, before I bought it. You can't really see it, but the DF32 emulator is the black box on top.)

I've got it in a DEC rack, together with my first PDP-8/I.

TODO: Current pictures of the 8/L.

In the winter of 2004, I acquired a BM08L memory expansion box to go with it, from Wai-Sun Chia. The BM08L is remarkable, as it interfaces the PDP-8/L to Omnibus memory boards, and allows expansion to the full 32K words.

In the summer of 2005, I acquired a second PDP-8/L, from Dan Lanciani, and a BA08 memory expansion to go with it, which allows an additional 4K of memory to be added.

The second PDP-8/L is mounted in a DEC rack, with the BA08, Charles' DF32 emulator, and my second PDP-8/I.
