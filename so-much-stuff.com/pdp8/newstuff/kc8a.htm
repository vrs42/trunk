#define TheTitle KC8A Re-Creation #include "header.h" Charles Morris and I have been trying to create an equivalent for the KC8A programmer&qt;s panel for the PDP-8/A. Here are some pictures of a prototype (before installing the blue wires).
This is what the front board looks like. There should be a metal sheet with cutouts for the LEDs and square buttons, but I haven&qt;t got one yet.
The other board attaches with two 40-conductor ribbon cables to the M8316 option board of the PDP-8/A. This board has most of the logic on it.

Visible on both boards are the DIP headers and little ribbon cables that couple the boards together. Both boards follow DEC&qt;s placement of the components quite closely, though these boards are a little "taller" than DEC&qt;s. The are intended to be electrically interchangeable, but not mechanically interchangeable.

This photo shows how the two boards are mounted with their solder sides facing each other, and coupled by the small ribbon cables. The spacers used in this picture are actually about twice as long as the ones that should be used, once everything is debugged. (They are the spacers that I have on hand.)
