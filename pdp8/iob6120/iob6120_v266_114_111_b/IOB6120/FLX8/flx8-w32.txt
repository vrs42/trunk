FLX8-W32 is a port of FLX8 that can read and write directly
to any Win32 block device.  For example a CF card can be put
in a CF adaptor/reader and mounted:

MOUNT I: /ID01 /PHYS	(assuming that I: is your CF reader)
PART 1
ZERO
WRITE myfile.pa /ASCII
DISMOUNT

Since the IOB allows hot-insertion of CF cards, this is a very
quick way to transfer files between the PC and the
SBC6120/IOB6120.
